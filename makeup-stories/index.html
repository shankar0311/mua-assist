About the Artist

"Every face tells a story."

Rajeshwari is a passionate and certified professional makeup artist based in Bangalore, India, with over 8 years of transformative experience in the beauty industry. Known for her meticulous attention to detail and deeply personalised approach, she has become one of the most sought-after makeup artists for bridal, editorial, and special-occasion looks.

Her philosophy is simple — every face tells a story, and makeup is the medium to bring that story to life. Whether it's a traditional South Indian bridal look adorned with flowers and gold, a contemporary dewy-skin bridal transformation, or a bold editorial concept, Rajeshwari approaches each project with unwavering creativity and care.
